# Pre-alpha readiness

No downloadable build has been published yet. We are holding the first binary
until these journeys are verified on a supported clean Mac:

- A new owner starts with Rhea, connects an account and hires the actual team.
- The app independently generates two different complete programmes with useful
  charts/tickers, voiced presenters and natural speaking/listening behaviour.
- Producer and standards decisions converge correctly. Current production can
  still pause on contradictory structured review results.
- Feedback, exact channel approval, original air, replay and restart recovery work
  without developer repair or duplicated purchases.
- Provider/source/runtime setup works without the developer’s local paths or
  private assets.
- The exact release is signed, notarized and tested after a real download.

Current development builds target macOS27.0 on Apple Silicon. Broader support is
unverified. Natural acting, staging and voice quality remain under active review.

The intended visual direction is stylized 2D/2.5D. Godot is being evaluated for
coherent actor states and blending; a runtime evaluation is not a claim that the
production app has adopted it or solved animation quality.

Please report your experience against the exact release version once downloads
are available. Avoid posting private studio content or credentials in issues.
